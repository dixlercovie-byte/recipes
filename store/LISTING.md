**Short description (80 max):** Save recipes, scale servings, convert grams to cups, and shop in one tap.

**Full description (4000 max):**

Recipes keeps all of your favorite recipes in one simple, fast app that works without an internet connection.

• Save your own recipes with ingredients, steps, notes, tags, cook time and servings
• Search by title, tag or ingredient, and star your favorites
• Scale servings up or down — ingredient amounts, including fractions, update automatically
• Built-in converter: grams to cups, tablespoons and teaspoons for common baking and cooking ingredients
• Add to shopping list: send a recipe's ingredients to your shopping list in one tap
• Copy ingredients, print a recipe, or export and import your whole collection as a backup file
• Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your recipes stay on your device.
