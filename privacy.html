Recipes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Recipes is a simple recipe-keeping app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or store any of your personal information on a server.

Where your recipes are stored

The recipes you add are saved only on your own device, in your browser or app's local storage. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your recipes are deleted. Use Export in the app to keep a backup file.

Sending ingredients to the shopping list

The "Add to shopping list" button opens the companion Shopping Lists web app and passes the ingredient names to it in the link. That app has its own optional live-sync feature; it is separate from Recipes and is not covered by this policy.

Third parties

Recipes does not include third-party SDKs, ads or analytics, and does not share data with anyone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
